# Under what conditions must the European Commission protect personal data during an inspection? Does the GDPR apply? Must documents be sealed on request?

**1.** The Commission's inspection decisions confer powers that in practice amount to search and seizure, enabling Commission agents to go through company premises, seal offices, block computer accounts and copy data, including personal data.
    inspections-eu-law-2nd--3_chapter_1_-_inspections_upon_decision_regulation_1_2003_article_20_4.md

**2.** During inspections, Commission agents are likely to collect, review and copy sensitive personal data without informing the individuals concerned or offering specific protection for the data.
    inspections-eu-law-2nd--3_chapter_1_-_inspections_upon_decision_regulation_1_2003_article_20_4.md

**3.** The Court has stressed the Commission's obligation to provide adapted protection of personal data collected during an inspection if such protection is requested.
    inspections-eu-law-2nd--3_chapter_1_-_inspections_upon_decision_regulation_1_2003_article_20_4.md

**4.** In Lagardère v Commission (T-1119/23), point (a) of recital 14 of the Commission's decision of 24 January 2024 states that documents unconnected with the applicant's commercial activities and containing sensitive personal data may be placed in a virtual data room under Regulation (EU) 2018/1725, and the General Court's judgment further notes that the contested decision provides procedural safeguards for communications covered by legal professional privilege and sensitive personal data.
    62023TJ1119.md

**5.** In Case C-258/23, the Court of Justice held that, in the absence of prior judicial authorisation, an inspection and any resulting seizure of personal data comply with Articles 7 and 8 of the Charter only where domestic legislation and practice provide adequate and sufficient safeguards against abuse and arbitrariness, taking the form of full ex post judicial review.
    62023CJ0258.md

**6.** Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 lays down rules on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data by the Union institutions and bodies, and it applies to the processing of personal data by all Union institutions and bodies.
    32018R1725.md

**7.** There is no exception from an undertaking's disclosure obligations toward the Commission for business secrets or for personal data protected under the GDPR or Regulation (EU) 2018/1725.
    eu-antitrust-enforcement--15_xiv_procedural_rights_and_obligations_of_third_parties_in_antitrust_investigations_and_proceedings_by_the_european_commission.md

**8.** Where the Commission restricts a data subject's rights under Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 during a competition investigation, it must record and register the necessity and proportionality of the restriction and keep the Data Protection Officer informed without undue delay, and the Officer may request a review of that restriction.
    32018D1927.md

**9.** When the Inspection Team seeks access to email inboxes or other data sets likely to contain personal information, the inspected company should immediately request a separate, specific process to protect that information, as foreseen by the Commission's own inspection guidance.
    inspections-eu-law-2nd--3_chapter_1_-_inspections_upon_decision_regulation_1_2003_article_20_4.md

**10.** In Case T-452/20, the relevant decision required that documents classified as 'Protected Documents' be transmitted to the Commission on a separate electronic medium and reviewed in a virtual data room accessible only to a limited investigative team in the presence of Facebook's lawyers, with an option for redacted transmission.
    62020TJ0452.md

**11.** In Case T-451/20, the judgment held that it is for the Commission, not the undertaking or its lawyers, to determine which documents are relevant, since allowing the undertaking to make that determination itself would risk undermining the Commission's investigative powers.
    62020TJ0451.md

**12.** In Les Mousquetaires and ITM Entreprises v Commission (C-682/20 P), the Court of Justice held that ex post judicial review of an inspection can counterbalance the absence of prior judicial review, provided appropriate redress is available through the totality of remedies, so as to satisfy Article 47 of the Charter for material seized during an inspection.
    62020CJ0682.md

**13.** Sealing of documents during a Commission inspection is not automatic on request: an inspected company must first raise serious doubts about the legally privileged or protected nature of a document before inspectors will place it in a sealed envelope pending resolution.
    competition-inspections-25-jurisdictions--7_european_union.md
